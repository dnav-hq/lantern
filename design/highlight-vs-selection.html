Telling a highlight from a selection

And renaming a category where you actually use it, rather than in Settings.

Your point 4 answers your point 2. If a highlighted verse carries its category name and a selected verse does not, the label is both the information you wanted and the thing that tells the two states apart. One move, two problems. Notes are untouched: the label uses exactly the treatment a note already uses — 10px, uppercase, letter-spaced, in the category's ink colour — so highlights read as the same family rather than a new species.

Naming a category where you use it

Not buried in Settings. Hover (or long-press) a row in the category menu and it becomes editable in place, the way Todoist and Notion do it. The same menu serves both the note tag picker and the highlight colour picker, so there is one place to learn.

What I would build from here

  • Highlight = category tint + the category name. Selection keeps its accent wash and left rule and gains nothing, so the two can never be confused.
  • Search-jump becomes a pulse that fades, as agreed — leaving exactly one persistent tint on the page.
  • Notes are untouched. Same rail, same label, same everything.
  • Renaming moves into the menu and comes out of Settings, so there is one place rather than two. The Settings section I built earlier gets removed.
  • Later, not now: an option to hide the category name on highlighted verses, for a reader who wants the tint alone.
