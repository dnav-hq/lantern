Note or highlight, as two visible choices

Tap a verse and the app asks which of the two things you are doing, instead of making you discover it. Three steps, then the two results side by side.

Where I was wrong. I made the existing Save button quietly become "Mark" when you had not typed anything. That is a hidden mode: it reads neatly in code and leaves you guessing in use. Two actions should look like two actions. It also rescues the tint. A highlighter look is confusing when the app applies it on your behalf, and obvious when you pressed a button called Highlight — so option B from the last round becomes the right answer, as long as it is told apart from the two transient washes (see the open question at the end).

What this changes, and the one thing still open

  • Two buttons, not a morphing one. "Write a note" opens the composer and the keyboard, exactly as today. "Highlight" never opens either.
  • Highlight is two taps and no typing. Colour is category, so the four you already have are the four swatches — and they carry your own names once renamed.
  • The tint is now the right answer. A highlighter look is confusing when the app decides it for you and obvious when you asked for it by name.
  • The Journal still needs fixing. A mark should show as its reference plus a short rule in the category colour — not a row containing the word "Marked".
The one open question: three washes. A tinted mark now shares the screen with two other tints — the verse you have selected (accent wash plus a left rule) and the verse a search result jumped you to. My proposal is that selection keeps its left rule as the thing that distinguishes it, and the search-jump changes from a resting wash to a brief pulse that fades — since it is telling you "here it is", not "this is a state you are in". That would leave exactly one persistent tint on the page, which is the mark. Worth your call, because it touches two things that already work.
