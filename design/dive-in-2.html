No numbers, no gradient, no list: a route that doubles back is no harder than one that does not, because only one leg is ever lit. What this drops from the first design.

  • The context-line classifier and the 66-book "who" table. The marks carry the relation; the reference carries the who.
  • The glimpse strip. Replaced by one line of real content with a count.
  • The verse-opens transition. The chapter never leaves; the shipped sheet motion stays.
  • Numbers, visit pairs, gradient, draw-in and the step list. Replaced by one lit leg and its verse.
  • The sea band. Coastlines are clipped as polygons, so the fill closes along the frame edge instead of a chord across the land.
  • Region stops that sit on a city. OpenBible places "Syria" on Damascus and "Judea" on Jerusalem; a region stop at a city's exact point is drawn as a label on the next leg, never as a return to the city.
